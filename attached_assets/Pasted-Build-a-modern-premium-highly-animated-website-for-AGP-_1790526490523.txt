Build a modern, premium, highly animated website for AGP Nursery Farm — a 
plant nursery in Pattoki, Pakistan (the country's biggest flower/plant 
growing hub), known on Instagram as @plants_paragon_pattoki. This should 
feel alive and emotionally engaging from the first second — think "boutique 
export-grade nursery with 219K Instagram followers," not "WooCommerce plant 
store."

POSITIONING
- Tagline: "Pakistan's No.1 Nursery Platform"
- Trusted by Landscapers & Exporters — Export-Grade, Mature, Premium Plants
- Delivers across Pakistan & to the Middle East
- Government-registered nursery business (Pattoki, District Kasur)

BRAND
- Colors: warm sunlit greens (#2F5233 dark green, #6B8E4E mid green) with 
  golden-amber accents (#D9A441) for CTAs, on a soft off-white background 
  (#FAF8F3). Avoid stark white or corporate blue.
- Typography: warm serif for headings (Fraunces or Playfair Display), clean 
  sans-serif for body text.
- Tone: warm, expert, a little poetic — not salesy.

IMAGES
- No gray placeholder boxes. Every image slot uses realistic, high-quality, 
  premium-looking images matching its actual subject (roses look like real 
  roses, topiary looks like real topiary, etc.) — mature, well-lit, 
  expensive-looking plants, not staged stock photos.
- Structure image components so they're easy to swap with real client 
  photos later.

MOTION (applies everywhere, not just the hero — this is a core requirement)
- Every section fades/slides in on scroll, staggered by 80-100ms per item 
  in any grid (category tiles, offer cards, gallery images)
- Hero background has a slow Ken Burns zoom/pan plus a soft parallax scroll 
  effect
- Cards and tiles get a hover lift (scale 1.03x, soft shadow, image zooms 
  slightly inside its frame)
- Floating chat button has a subtle idle pulse/breathing animation
- All animations 200-400ms, smooth and snappy — never slow or laggy

HERO SECTION
- Full-width, Ken Burns/parallax background of lush greenery
- Headline: "Your garden is missing something." then "Pattoki's finest 
  plants, delivered to your door."
- Subtext: "219,000+ people already follow AGP Nursery Farm. Export-grade, 
  mature, premium plants — grown in Pattoki, delivered across Pakistan and 
  the Middle East."
- Live-counting "219,000+ Followers" animation on load
- Primary CTA: "Chat to Order on WhatsApp" (wa.me link, amber/gold)
- Secondary CTA: "Find My Plant" → scrolls to the Plant Match quiz

PLANT MATCH QUIZ — designed as a guided conversation, not a form
- One question at a time; selecting an answer auto-advances after ~300ms, 
  no "Next" button
- Slide/fade transition between questions (current slides out left, next 
  slides in right); progress bar visibly fills
- Each answer option has a press animation (scale down/up + highlight + 
  brief checkmark) on click
- Between last question and results: a 1-1.5s "Finding your matches..." 
  state with a small animated sprout/pulsing dots
- Results reveal with staggered card animation, opening with a personalized 
  line built from their answers (e.g. "Since you want something flowering 
  for full sun, here's what we'd recommend:")
- Ambient subtle background animation inside the quiz panel (drifting leaf 
  particles or slow gradient shift)
- Question wording, conversational and warm, not survey-like:
  - "Picture the spot in your mind — is this plant going indoors or out?"
  - "Now think about that spot — does the sun really hit it, or is it 
    mostly shade?"
  - "Last one — what feeling are you going for? Lush jungle energy, 
    colorful blooms, or something you barely have to think about?"
- Each result card has an "Ask About This" WhatsApp button
